Keep Your Data Safe

Back up your data and clear when needed

The site might not work correctly sometimes. If that happens, back up your data and clear your cookies/site data.

Confused? Click here for help

How to clear site data:

  1. Click the lock icon (or tune icon) in your browser's address bar
  2. Click "Cookies and site data"
  3. Click "Manage on-device site data"
  4. Click the trash icon next to the site data to delete it
  5. Refresh the page

Remember to export your data first!
